**Terms.** The Kaggle page states no license: it is a regrouping of the data of the ICDAR 2019 Robust Reading Challenge on Scanned Receipts OCR and Information Extraction (rrc.cvc.uab.es/?ch=13), released by the organizers for research. So here it is used for evaluation only. The receipts, their transcriptions and the per-receipt outputs of the models stay in `external/` and in `results/sroie_*`, both git-ignored; only aggregate numbers are published, with a citation of the challenge.

**Role.** The test split (347 receipts) is a frozen test set for the compression tracks, designated on 2026-10-12 before any model was run on it; reads are logged in `LEDGER.md`. `generators/make_sroie.py` converts it to this repository's format. The train split is free for tuning.

## Gazzetta Ufficiale della Repubblica Italiana, Serie Generale

- **Source.** www.gazzettaufficiale.it: the archive page of each year (`/ricercaArchivioCompleto/serie_generale/YEAR`) lists the issues; each issue's PDF is at `/eli/gu/YYYY/MM/DD/NUMBER/sg/pdf`. Downloaded on 2026-10-14 by `generators/make_gu.py`, one request a second, into `external/gu_src/`.
- **Terms.** Official acts of the State and of public administrations are excluded from copyright (law 633/1941, art. 5). The site publishes no terms of use that we found. The PDFs and the pages built from them stay in `external/`, which is not in git, as for every third-party set.
- **Use.** 42 issues, 6 a year from 2019 to 2025, drawn with seed 20261014; 4 pages from each (never the cover). Issues alternate: 21 to `external/gu_dev` (84 pages), 21 to `external/gu_test` (84 pages). Ground truth = the PDF's text layer, hyphenated words joined; entities by pattern (dates, act numbers, amounts). Born-digital pages rendered at 150 dpi: real Italian typesetting, but clean renders, not scans. Two limits found on 2026-10-15: the running head of some issues is set in small caps whose text layer comes out as Malayalam and Sinhala letters, so it is garbled in the ground truth too (entities are taken from the body only, `make_gu.py`); and since the ground truth is the text layer, this set cannot measure `glmocr/pdf.py`.
- **Training.** `external/gu_train` (`make_gu.py --train`): 40 pages with amounts of a million or more or dot leaders, from 22 other issues, never one of gu_dev or gu_test. Used by the fine-tuning runs op3 and op4, which were not released.

## DocAtlas, Italian pages

- **Source.** `ahmedheakl/docatlas_instruct` on Hugging Face, CC BY 4.0: pages of real documents found on the web, rendered from their DOCX, with the text of every block (doctags and markdown). The 2356 Italian pages (571 documents) were downloaded by `download_docatlas_it.py` into `~/Documents/git/datasets/huggingface/data/docatlas_it`, and made to look scanned by `scan_fx.py` into `docatlas_it_scan` (both outside this repository).
- **Use.** Test: 40 documents, one page each, clean and scanned (`generators/make_docatlas.py`). The 40 documents are listed in `external/docatlas_test_docs.json`; any training drawn from DocAtlas must leave them out. Training: 70 pages of other documents (`make_docatlas.py --train`), used by the fine-tuning run op3, not released.
- **Limits of the ground truth**, found on 2026-10-15: blanks come as markdown escapes (`\_\_\_`), some forms are one line, and the order is the DOCX's, not always the page's. Harmless for the test entities (long words and codes); not fit as training answers until cleaned.

## Omni Parse Bench, reduced subset (`external/opb_sub`)

Datalab's document-parsing benchmark (github.com/datalab-to/omni_parse_bench; dataset datalab-to/omni_parse_bench on Hugging Face), 2,937 pages, 16,288 yes/no tests. Scorer: Apache-2.0 (`omni-parse-bench` 0.2.0, installed in `work/opb_venv`). Tests: CC BY 4.0. Pages: under the license of each source, listed in the dataset's `licenses/`. Nothing leaves `external/`. `generators/make_opb.py` takes 80 PDF pages, 8 from each of ten suites of real documents (seed 20261015), European languages only where the suite is multilingual; 184 text tests (present 151, order 30, repeat 3). Some tests come from the PDF itself (origin `text_layer`, `extractor_agreement`): they are reported apart when the PDF text layer is measured. Their published GLM-OCR result (81.5) runs the vendor SDK: layout detection, then each region read at 200 dpi with a repetition penalty; the pages here are read whole, so the numbers are not comparable with theirs.
