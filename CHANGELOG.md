# Changelog

Versions of the released model and of the `glmocr` package go together. Dates are those of the measurements.

## v1.5.0 - 2026-11-12

Same MLX and GGUF weights as v1.4.0. New: `glmocr infer` reads the top and bottom bands of a page as well, unless the page is turned.

- **Bands, on by default in `infer`.** After the whole page, the top 16% and the bottom 14% are read on their own, and the lines of theirs that the whole page lacks are put into it (`glmocr/bands.py`): the letterhead and the closing lines a whole-page reading loses. Lines a band returns that are not text of the page (a markdown fence, a description of the image, a bare count) are dropped. `--no-bands` reads the whole page only. `glmocr run --bands` measures it. With `--deskew` or `--regions` no band is read.
- **The guard: an orientation classifier.** No band is read on a page that PP-LCNet_x1_0_doc_ori (PaddlePaddle, Apache-2.0) is sure, at 0.8 or more, is turned (`glmocr/orient.py`). It runs in numpy, 26 ms a page on a CPU, with no new dependency; its answers match the PyTorch implementation on 1243 of 1243 images, and Phase 1's PaddleX numbers (159/160 rotations of `hard`, 475/480 of `held`). The page is not straightened: a turned page is still read on its side (README, Limits).
- **Measured on test sets, MLX** (docs/LEDGER.md, 2026-11-12, every question written before its read), against the release's whole page: XFUND Italian forms 77.7% to 79.9%, +2.20 [+0.89, +4.02]; ocr-bench-v1 91.8% to 96.6%, +4.78 [+3.10, +6.59], CER 6.9% to 2.9%; Omni Parse Bench with the text layer (bands on the 51 pages the model reads) 75.5% to 77.2% of 184 tests, +3.1 [+0.0, +6.9] per page, not worse; no page lost on any. The two steps never measured there were read too: the fine-tuned model alone gives 91.8% on ocr-bench-v1 (no page loops, so the retry changes nothing) and 58.2% on Omni Parse Bench.
- **Measured, MLX.** On 200 letters no model had read (`itdoc_15`), against the whole page: +3.69 [+2.67, +4.83] entity recall, 0 pages lost, 65 gained, pages whose last line is lost 16 to 1, CER 3.03% to 3.26%, time +20%. On the dev sets never worse: ocr-bench training half +3.44 [+2.29, +4.75], XFUND Italian forms +1.09 [+0.72, +1.55], phone photographs (MDPBench) +0.82 [+0.27, +1.57], Gazzetta +0.17, `hard` +0.21. On MDPBench's test photographs the CER rises by 1.0 point (22.8% to 23.8%), recall +0.57 [+0.19, +1.10]. `glmocr run --bands` on `hard` reproduces the merge measured from the saved readings byte for byte (40 of 40 pages, 76 of 76 bands).
- **GGUF.** The bands run with the llama.cpp reader too, and in the Docker image on linux/amd64 (one page read with both bands and the classifier, 15% more time on a CPU); they are not measured there.
- **A loose entity check, beside the exact one.** `glmocr score` and `compare` also report entity recall with typography folded (one apostrophe, one quote sign, one dash; tick boxes and fill-in underscores dropped), never instead of the exact check, which the verdicts stay on. On XFUND Italian forms 61% of the entities missed by one or two characters were typography (curly apostrophes, tick boxes); accents, digits, letters and the decimal notation stay strict (`metrics.found_loose`).
- `rapidfuzz` is declared (it came with jiwer, same version).

## v1.4.0 - 2026-11-11

Same MLX and GGUF weights as v1.3.0 (same SHA-256). New: a third reading for the pages whose loop the second reading does not break.

- **Stage-2 loop retry.** When a page loops, the reader reads it again with `repetition_penalty` 1.1, as since v1.1.0. That penalty only looks back 20 tokens, so a repeated paragraph or clause longer than that, or a dot leader, can survive it. Now, when the second reading is still cut by the loop stop, a third reading uses penalty 1.3 over the last 256 tokens, and the reading kept is chosen as before (`engine.keep_retry`). The loop stop also catches repetitions of up to 600 characters (400 before). A page that does not loop, or whose loop the second reading breaks, is read exactly as in v1.3.0. First measured on development sets (2026-11-04), then confirmed on test sets (2026-11-11).
- **Measured, MLX** (docs/LEDGER.md, "Stage-2 loop retry on seven test sets", question and thresholds written before the reads). On seven test sets, 16 distinct pages could change: 5 gained (a German form +7 entities, two scientific pages +38 and +30, two Italian documents +6 and +5), 2 lost (an Italian page that repeats near-identical sentences by design, -2 entities, an IBAN and a code; a US rate-filing table, -1 benchmark test), 9 unchanged; net +84 entities. Time added on a page read again: 10 to 71 s, median 20 s. On the development sets: XFUND Italian forms +0.28 [+0.00, +0.87], Gazzetta Ufficiale +0.95 [+0.00, +2.80].
- **GGUF.** The same logic runs in the llama.cpp reader (`glmocr/llama.py`). It was measured on development sets only (on the Gazzetta, one dot-leader page from 0 to 3 of 3 entities; one page still cut), not on the test sets: no test-set claim is made for it.
- **Fixed: the documented install.** Since 2026-11-03 `pyproject.toml` and `uv.lock` pinned Pillow 13.0.0 while `requirements.lock` pinned 12.3.0, so `pip install -r requirements.lock -e .` could not resolve. Back to Pillow 12.3.0 everywhere: every number of this repository was measured with it, and Pillow 13 changes what the model is given on images the processor resizes (a phone photograph read with 13.0.0 differs from the 22nd character on, while its decoded pixels are the same; 19 of 20 pages read alike). Moving to Pillow 13 is a change of its own, to be measured. certifi and filelock keep their updates.
- **Fixed.** The stage-2 test ran the MLX reader, so it failed where MLX is not installed (Linux, Docker); it now checks the llama.cpp reader everywhere and the MLX reader on Apple Silicon.
- **Measured, not released** (docs/ROADMAP.md, "After v1.3.0"): reading the top and bottom bands of a page apart and merging them halves the misses where a page loses its letterhead or closing lines (ocr-bench training half, 151 to 80 misses at +23% time), but on real phone photographs a band that frames no text is described rather than read, which raises the character error rate: not adopted. The whole page at 1.33x: not worth twice the time. A 6-bit decoder: not worse, 123 MB lighter, not released (size is the last priority).
- **New page sets.** The Italian pages of MDPBench's public set (real phone photographs, Apache-2.0, `generators/make_mdpbench.py`), split into a dev and a test set by document; a blind correspondence set commissioned from a model that knew nothing of the project.

## v1.3.0 - 2026-10-17

Same MLX weights as v1.0.0 (same SHA-256). New: a GGUF of the same fine-tuned weights, and a harness that runs off Apple Silicon.

- **GGUF, a portable variant.** The release's weights in llama.cpp's GGUF (Q8_0 language model, f16 vision projector, 1819 MB), built with llama.cpp b11707 from the same fine-tuned bf16 checkpoint: [fabriziosalmi/GLM-OCR-ita-GGUF](https://huggingface.co/fabriziosalmi/GLM-OCR-ita-GGUF). `glmocr infer|run` reads a `.gguf` or a `-GGUF` repository through `llama-server` (`glmocr/llama.py`) with the same prompt, loop stop and retry; off Apple Silicon it is the default model. Not shown equivalent to the MLX release on development sets (docs/ROADMAP.md, "Comparison with the MLX release"): better on synthetic Italian, +1.8 [+0.7, +3.0]; not worse on XFUND Italian train and ocr-bench; not shown on chatgpt A (-0.2 [-1.6, +0.9]), the Gazzetta (-1.1 [-4.0, +1.0]) and synthetic English (-1.2 [-3.1, +0.9]), each loss one page. 5 to 20% faster on a Mac mini.
- **Linux, Windows, Docker.** MLX is installed on Apple Silicon only. The memory fences read `/proc/meminfo` and a container's cgroup limit on Linux, and the Windows API on Windows (written, not tested). `Dockerfile`: llama.cpp's server image and uv, pinned by digest, glmocr from `uv.lock`; built and read on linux/amd64 and linux/arm64. `GLMOCR_WORK`, `GLMOCR_REPO`: where an installed package keeps its files.
- **Deskew, off by default.** `--deskew DEG [--trim]` straightens a page turned by at least DEG degrees (`glmocr/deskew.py`). Better on skewed stress pages; worse at 1 degree on spark C and SROIE, so not on by default.
- **Fixed.** A phone photo stored with an EXIF orientation reached the model on its side when glmocr resized, straightened or cropped it, and always through llama.cpp (found on real receipts, 2026-10-17).
- **Region mode.** `glmocr infer --regions` reads a page after GLM-OCR's own pipeline: its SDK's layout detector and formatter (Z.ai, Apache-2.0, unmodified, in their own PyTorch environment: `GLMOCR_LAYOUT_PYTHON` or `--layout-python`), each region read by the MLX reader with the prompt of its kind. Headers, footers, page numbers and footnotes are read unless `--drop-margins`. Not the default: worse on real Italian forms (docs/RESULTS.md, "Since v1.2.0").
- **Measured.** OmniDocBench `omnidoc_test2` by regions: overall (formulas by edit distance) 93.7 against 64.1 read whole; v1.1.0 not worse than the original on English or Chinese text. ocr-bench-v1 test half: +8.1 [+4.2, +13.6] over the original.
- **New.** `generators/make_ocrbench.py`; `experiments/omnidoc_compare.py` takes the set, its languages, formulas, tables and page types; `glmocr score` reads runs made by regions.
- **Training answers checked against the teacher's reading** (`experiments/ft_targets.py`, checks in `glmocr/metrics.py`): a page is left out when its ground truth lacks what the teacher reads, holds fewer tick boxes or blanks, is in another order, or flattens a form into one line. `--max-missing 2 --min-order 0` gives back the answers of op1 and op2 byte for byte.
- **DocAtlas training pages** without markdown escapes (`make_docatlas.py --train`); the test sets are unchanged.

## v1.2.0 - 2026-10-15

Same weights as v1.0.0 and v1.1.0 (same SHA-256); PDFs are read differently.

- **PDF text layer.** `glmocr infer file.pdf` reads each page from its own text layer where it passes four checks, and with the model only where it does not (`glmocr/pdf.py`; `--mode auto|ocr|text`, `--pages`). The idea and the checks come from Indra OCR by Datalab ([github.com/datalab-to/indra](https://github.com/datalab-to/indra), `indra_ocr/textlayer.py`, Apache-2.0; `NOTICE`), adapted. Two changes are ours: Latin text whose font map comes out as another script (the Gazzetta Ufficiale's small caps) is not trusted, and characters beyond the BMP are joined from pdfium's two UTF-16 halves before they are checked.
- **Measured** (docs/RESULTS.md, "Release v1.2.0"): on 80 real PDF pages of Omni Parse Bench (Datalab), scored by its own scorer, 76.1% of the text tests against 62.5% for v1.1.0, +12.2 [+5.8, +19.6]; +8.2 [+2.0, +16.3] on the tests not derived from the PDF. v1.1.0 against the original on the same pages: +6.9 [+2.2, +12.4]. Same weights, measured again: XFUND val German, Spanish, French, Portuguese all better than the original; 40 real Italian documents of DocAtlas not worse, clean or scanned.
- **New.** `experiments/pdf_eval.py` (a page set read from its PDFs), `experiments/opb_score.py` and `generators/make_opb.py` (a reduced Omni Parse Bench), `generators/make_docatlas.py`, `make_gu.py` (Gazzetta Ufficiale), `split_chatgpt.py`; `ft_targets.py` leaves out a page whose ground truth lacks what the teacher reads. Dependency added: `pypdfium2`.
- **Not released.** Fine-tuning runs op2 to op4 (comb fields; blanks, tick boxes and large amounts) are worse than v1.1.0 on XFUND Italian train with the retry (-2.1 to -2.3): `docs/ROADMAP.md`.
- **Corrected.** The loop threshold's "gap" holds on synthetic pages only: on real pages right outputs also compress to 21-27% (docs/RESULTS.md, failure modes).

## v1.1.0 - 2026-10-14

Same weights as v1.0.0 (same SHA-256); the reading changes.

- **Retry on loops.** A page that loops is read a second time with mlx-vlm's `repetition_penalty=1.1`; the second reading is kept unless it loops too and returns less text (`engine.keep_retry`). On by default in `glmocr infer` (`--retry-penalty 0` turns it off); off in `glmocr run` unless asked. Penalty chosen on XFUND Italian train (149 real forms, dev): 1.05, 1.1, 1.2 tried; 1.1 closes 5 of 6 loops.
- **Measured** (docs/RESULTS.md, "Release v1.1.0"): on XFUND Italian val (50 real forms, test) +3.1 [+1.3, +5.9] over the original, filled-in values +1.1 [-0.9, +3.2], loops 2 -> 0. v1.0.0 alone was +0.3 [-3.0, +2.9] there, with two new loops on comb fields. On an OmniDocBench subset (200 pages, official evaluator, text blocks) English is not shown either way (-0.3 [-2.7, +1.7]) and Chinese is worse (-4.8 [-10.6, -0.9]), with or without the retry.
- **New.** Converters for XFUND and an OmniDocBench subset, the official OmniDocBench evaluator wrapper (`experiments/omnidoc_eval.py`), the retry on saved runs (`experiments/loop_retry.py`), per-type comparison. Training pages for comb fields, not yet used: the official F24 filled with made-up values, twelve printable Italian forms, continuous-severity degradation.

## v1.0.0 - 2026-10-14

First release of weights: `GLM-OCR-ita-mlx-8bit` on Hugging Face (`fabriziosalmi/GLM-OCR-ita-mlx-8bit`).

- **Model.** `zai-org/GLM-OCR` (revision `2e85a62`) with LoRA adapter `op1` (rank 8, decoder only, 90 steps, learning rate 1e-5, 91 synthetic pages the original misread) fused into the bf16 weights, then quantized: encoder, decoder, embeddings and output head at 8 bits, groups of 64. 1184 MB, `model.safetensors` SHA-256 `f98e8f0c4e26903f71cccd2c115add8000a150141963da6f7b51ed0624d61ccd`.
- **Measured** (docs/RESULTS.md, "Release v1.0.0"): on the `spark/itdoc_b` test set Italian +3.7 [+2.4, +5.1] and English +7.1 [+4.1, +10.5] against the original; not worse on `chatgpt/itdoc_c` (+0.7 [+0.0, +1.4]) and on 100 real SROIE receipts (+0.0 [-1.2, +1.5]). Peak memory 2.10 GB against 3.16, 72 generated tokens/s against 65.
- **Package.** `glmocr infer` reads with the release by default, at full resolution. `glmocr quant` takes `--src` and `--out`; `experiments/ft_merge.py` fuses an adapter and checks the result against mlx-vlm's own fuse; `experiments/compare_lang.py` compares by language. `tests/test_release.py` recomputes the release table where the page sets are on the machine.
- **Fixed on the way.** Every read uses the official prompt (`Text Recognition:` with no `/nothink` and no empty think block); fine-tuning computes its loss with the causal mask that mlx-vlm 0.7.6 leaves out ([mlx-vlm#2509](https://github.com/Blaizzy/mlx-vlm/issues/2509)); the reader stops a repetition loop as it starts.
